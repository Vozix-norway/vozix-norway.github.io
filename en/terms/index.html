2. What Vozix is

Vozix is a photo app for Android. You take photos and videos, add keywords and descriptions, and find your photos again with search, folders and the map. You can record trips as tracks and make films of them. Everything is stored on your phone. Vozix has no account and no cloud storage of its own.

3. Free and PRO

Vozix is free to download and use. In the free version you can organise up to 250 photos and videos, meaning you give them keywords or a description, put them in a folder or change their location. The limit is a total, so if you delete an organised photo, you do not get the slot back. Photos that are already organised can always be viewed and edited. You will be told before you reach the limit.

Vozix PRO is a subscription that removes the limit and adds more features, such as Secure Folder. What PRO includes is shown on the PRO page in the app.

PRO also includes a custom theme and adding a location to photos that have none.

  • Purchase and payment: PRO is bought through Google Play, and payment goes to Google Play. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy.
  • Renewal: The subscription renews automatically every month or every year, depending on what you chose, until you cancel it.
  • No lock-in: You can cancel at any time in Google Play (Subscriptions). You keep PRO until the end of the period you have paid for, and you will not be charged again.
  • Right of withdrawal: PRO is available immediately. Google Play asks you to agree that delivery starts immediately and to confirm that you then lose the right of withdrawal (Norwegian Cancellation Act, section 22 (n)). You can request a refund through Google Play under Google's policies. This does not limit your rights under the law if something is wrong with PRO.
  • Price changes: If the price changes, Google Play will notify you in good time, and you can cancel before the new price applies.
  • New phone: Sign in with the same Google account, and Vozix gets your PRO access from Google Play.
  • When PRO ends: Your photos, videos and keywords stay where they are. You return to the free version and its limit for new photos. Secure Folder can still be unlocked, and you can view the photos and move them out again, but not add new ones.

6. Secure Folder and password

  • Secure Folder is locked with your fingerprint, face or your phone's screen lock. The photos are kept in Vozix's private storage, hidden from other apps and from your phone's gallery.
  • When you move a photo or video to Secure Folder, Android asks you to confirm that the copy in your phone's regular gallery is deleted. If you confirm, the photo then exists only in Vozix. If you decline, the copy stays in your gallery. If you uninstall Vozix without having made a backup, the photos in Secure Folder are gone for good.
  • In the backup, photos in Secure Folder are encrypted with a password you choose yourself. The password is not stored anywhere, and neither we nor anyone else can recover it. It is your responsibility to keep it safe. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe away from your phone, for example in a password manager or on paper at home.
  • Vozix can remind you to make a backup every week, every month or every three months. You turn this on under Settings → Backup.
  • We are not responsible for photos that are lost because you forgot the password, or because you uninstalled the app or lost your phone without a valid backup.
